About Nassau County Appliance Repair

Your trusted partner for home appliance repair throughout Nassau County, Long Island.

Our Story

Nassau County Appliance Repair was founded with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable appliance repair service to Long Island families. We understand that a broken appliance disrupts your daily life, and we're committed to getting your household back to normal as quickly as possible.

Our team consists of factory-trained technicians who share a passion for solving problems and helping people. We invest continuously in training to stay current with the latest appliance technologies, from smart home integrations to high-efficiency systems.

Our Values

Honesty: We provide accurate diagnoses and transparent pricing. If a repair isn't worth the investment, we'll tell you.

Expertise: Our technicians receive ongoing training from major manufacturers. We understand how your appliances work and how to fix them properly.

Respect: We treat your home like our own. Shoe covers, drop cloths, and thorough cleanup are standard practice.

Reliability: When we schedule an appointment, we show up on time. When we quote a price, we honor it.

Service Area

We proudly serve all of Nassau County, from the North Shore communities of Great Neck and Manhasset to the South Shore towns of Freeport and Long Beach. Our central location allows us to respond quickly to service calls throughout the county.
