Dryer Repair Services

A malfunctioning dryer wastes energy and time. Whether your dryer won't heat, takes forever to dry clothes, or makes concerning noises, our technicians have the expertise to fix it. We service both gas and electric dryers from all major brands.

Common Dryer Problems We Fix

  • Not heating
  • Taking too long to dry
  • Tumbling issues
  • Making grinding or squealing noises
  • Shutting off early
  • Burning smell
  • Won't start
  • Drum not turning
