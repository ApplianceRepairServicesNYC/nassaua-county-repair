Washing Machine Repair Services

Your washing machine handles hundreds of loads each year. When it breaks down, laundry piles up fast. Our Nassau County washer repair technicians diagnose and fix all washing machine problems, from simple drain clogs to complex control board failures.

Common Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • Not spinning or agitating
  • Leaking water onto floor
  • Won't drain properly
  • Making loud noises during cycles
  • Not filling with water
  • Stopping mid-cycle
  • Door won't lock or unlock
  • Leaving clothes too wet
