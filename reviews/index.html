Frequently Asked Questions

Are these reviews from real customers?

Yes. All reviews reflect genuine feedback from homeowners in Nassau County who have used our appliance repair services. We encourage customers to share their experiences on Google after each completed repair.

Do you service high-end appliance brands?

Absolutely. Our technicians receive specialized training for premium brands including Sub-Zero, Viking, Thermador, Wolf, Miele, and Bosch. We carry manufacturer-approved parts and follow factory repair procedures.

How quickly can you respond to a service request?

We offer same-day appointments throughout Nassau County for most repair requests. Our dispatch team works to schedule technicians efficiently across Garden City, Hempstead, Valley Stream, Great Neck, and surrounding areas.

Yes. All of our appliance repair technicians are fully licensed, insured, and background-checked. We maintain EPA Section 608 certification for refrigerant handling and complete ongoing manufacturer training programs.
